<header class="page-heading">
  <p class="eyebrow">Privacy</p>
  <h1>Your meeting data stays between you, macOS and the portal you choose.</h1>
  <p>Viamiora has no Viamiora account, advertising, analytics or operated data service.</p>
</header>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Portal data and credentials</h2>
  <p>Viamiora connects only to the HTTPS Mividas portal address that you enter. Sign-in credentials are stored in your macOS Keychain when you choose to save them. Session cookies stay in memory for the active session.</p>
  <p>Meeting titles, times, participants, joining links and portal booking forms are exchanged with your chosen portal so the app can show, create and manage meetings. Viamiora does not relay that data through a YunaBraska service.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Optional macOS permissions</h2>
  <p>Calendar access lets Viamiora show recognised meeting links and consider busy times while suggesting a booking. Contacts access suggests participant email addresses. Camera and microphone access are used only when you join a meeting that needs them. Viamiora does not record or retain camera or microphone media.</p>
  <p>You can deny or revoke each permission in macOS System Settings. The related feature then remains unavailable rather than using another hidden source.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Local data and support</h2>
  <p>Portal settings, appearance choices and manually entered participant addresses are stored locally on your Mac. System contacts are not copied into that local address cache. You can remove portal accounts and uninstall Viamiora at any time.</p>
  <p>For help, open an issue through <a href="{{ site.support }}">Viamiora support</a>. Do not include credentials, joining links or other private meeting information in a public issue.</p>
  <p>Last updated: 19 November 2026.</p>
</section>
